How to download a YouTube video

Whether you want the full video, just the audio, or a specific resolution, here's exactly how to do it in under a minute.

Find the video on YouTube

Open the YouTube app or website and find the video you want to save. It can be a regular video, a Short, or an unlisted video you already have the link to.

Copy the video link

Tap the Share button under the video and choose Copy link. On desktop, you can copy the URL straight from the address bar. The link usually looks like https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=… or https://youtu.be/….

Paste it into YTGrab

Head back to the YTGrab homepage and paste the link into the input box. There's nothing to install and no account to create — the box is ready as soon as the page loads.

Tap Download

Press the Download button and wait a moment while the video is fetched. This is the only step where you need to wait — everything after this happens instantly.

Choose your format and quality

Pick MP4 for the full video, or MP3 if you only need the audio — a podcast clip, a song, a lecture. Each option shows its file size upfront, so there are no surprises.

Save the file to your device

Tap the download icon next to your chosen format. The file saves straight to your phone or computer's downloads folder, ready to watch offline in any player.

Good to know

Tips for a smoother download

Use a stable connection

Larger files, like 1080p or 4K video, take longer on a weak connection. Switch to Wi-Fi for big downloads where possible.

Pick the right format first

If you only need the sound, choose MP3 rather than MP4 — it downloads much faster and takes up far less space.

One video at a time

For now, paste one link per download. Support for saving entire playlists in one go is on the roadmap.
